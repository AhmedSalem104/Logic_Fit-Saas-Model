# Decision 2.13 — CRM Follow-up

**Status:** APPROVED  
**Authority:** Phase 2 Final Gap Resolution  
**Scope:** CRM activities and follow-ups.

## Decision

Every follow-up may contain due date, type, note, owner, and completed state. Overdue follow-ups are visible in the CRM list/timeline and are determined by server time/date semantics. Completion is an auditable state change. Reminder delivery is not a required paid/external integration; notifications use the in-app adapter contract.
